# OG product direction

Build a polished luxury property-management experience for Devonshire Leasing Agency: ivory, navy and restrained champagne accents, clear typography, accessible controls, generous spacing and plain language. Prefer a short monthly workflow with visible exceptions and complete owner packets across all units.

The long-term roadmap includes leasing, resident/owner/vendor portals, maintenance, document delivery, accounting, bank reconciliation and reporting comparable in scope to AppFolio and QuickBooks. Deliver these incrementally; never imply unfinished integrations or financial capabilities are available.

Protect financial integrity: verify current external transaction status, identify the QBO company and bank by ID, preserve immutable audit history and approved source versions, enforce idempotency, distinguish printing from payment, and require verified original voids before replacement checks. Never issue or adjust money automatically. Reset monthly estimates and carry forward verified balances.

This repository is public. Keep owner statements, backups, receipts, reconciliation records and credentials outside source control. Published tests must use synthetic financial records.
