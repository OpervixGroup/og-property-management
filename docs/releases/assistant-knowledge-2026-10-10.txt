Security/limitations
No external generative model is configured or called. This is an enhanced knowledge-driven chat and guided workflow assistant; open-ended model-generated conversation requires a separately configured AI provider. Live bank/QBO data is not queried. Model tests preserve unit/property scoping and deny restricted balances. Existing APIs, financial posting rules, tenancy permissions and data storage are unchanged.
Marketing, automated AI prospecting and lender collaboration remain planned. Screening, signatures and listings use manual evidence. Mail/bank/payment integrations depend on verified company-specific provider setup.
