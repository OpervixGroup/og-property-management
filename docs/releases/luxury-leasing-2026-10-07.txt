OG LUXURY LEASING WORKFLOW — October 7, 2026
Status: Built for review. GoDaddy publication is on hold at the user's request.

WORKFLOW
1. Leasing > Vacancies: verify occupancy and manual marketing status for the selected accounting month. Unknown occupancy never becomes a vacancy automatically.
2. Guest cards: record contact, interested unit, source, assigned colleague, move-in date, follow-up and notes. Open the unit number for its global record.
3. Showings: choose guest, date, Chicago time and host. Record completion or cancellation. Conflicting unit/host appointments at the same time are blocked.
4. Applications: create a draft from the guest card. Save Submitted, then Under review. A person records Approved, Denied or Withdrawn with a date and reason. Reviewed decisions stay intact.
5. Leases: approved applications create one draft. Enter dates and exact contract rent. Link the resident, attach your approved signed lease, then use Rent & residents > Occupancy, prospects & leases to verify signature and activate. Activation retains existing tenant/lease validation; it creates no payments.
6. Renewals: active leases ending within 90 days appear automatically. Prepare one separate draft starting after the prior lease. Verify resident term and signature before activation. The original signed contract is preserved.
7. Metrics: property-filtered saved-record counts, lead sources, and a CSV summary. Application counts are cumulative; vacancy reflects the selected month; showings are upcoming and renewals use the next 90 days.

NAVIGATION AND APPEARANCE
Ivory, navy and champagne throughout. Six stages, clear tasks that change with the section, search/property controls, reusable global unit dialog (unit, tenant, owner, vendors, leases, maintenance), read-only amount source dialogs in common tables, and print-safe links.

SAFETY AND ACCESS
Existing authenticated workspace save, revision checks, audit history and management permissions remain in place. No financial posting is introduced. No QBO payment, screening decision or AppFolio record was changed. New application/showing records are optional for existing backups. Reviewed records and completed showing history cannot be removed or overwritten. Duplicate applications, application leases and renewals are blocked. Signed lease and rent history controls remain enforced.
