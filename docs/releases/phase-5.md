# Phase 5 — owner review, distributions and monthly close

Owners need a single review of their unit deductions and support before distributions, and a permanent close that preserves unpaid tenant balances. Accounting now includes Owner bills & monthly close: owner filter, itemized Letter PDF, unit bill/support packages, distribution shortcut and completed contribution records.

A final month requires zero-difference rent clearing, reviewed fee agreements/active QBO GL/source references, approved statements, each owner unit settled, required manual repair support and external operating/escrow reconciliation references. The $10,000 DLA income minimum remains an internal shortfall flag only. No additional owner fee is invented.

Close history and financial source records are immutable. Save a documented reopen before corrections. The final ZIP includes owner packages separately from private DLA close/tenant arrears records. Open the following month from the completed close: owner opening balances and allocation start at zero, fees carry forward for review, CPS and cash sources require a new review; tenant charges and unpaid balances remain on their original ledger.

No migration, dependency, bank transfer, external QBO posting or email sending is introduced. Actual bank references are management attestations, not automated bank reconciliation. Live integration remains Phase 6. Archives use the existing private download route and its 100 MB package limit; unit/owner packages are also available separately.

Validation: 225 automated assertions/scenarios including 18 new close tests; production build, authenticated HTTP checks for trusted reviewer/timestamps, immutable sources, missing support, role denial, idempotency and opening next month; disposable-provider browser contribution/clearing/close/download/month rollover; Letter print visual QA. No QA provider or test records may be deployed to the live database.
