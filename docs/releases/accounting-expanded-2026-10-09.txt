OG ACCOUNTING EXPANSION — 2026-10-09

Scope: core manual accounting workflows, not full AppFolio parity.

Implemented:
1. Itemized supplier bills and credits, service dates, original source references, immutable approval history, liability posting, and recorded-payment/credit applications.
2. Bulk bill/credit CSV import with atomic validation, account/unit resolution, duplicate checks, itemized previews and selected-document approval.
3. Monthly recurring bill/credit templates, pause/resume history, one preparation per template/month, separate approval and posting. The UI defaults to next month and clears prior invoice service dates.
4. Existing tenant charge/receipt/credit source posting to company AR/income/bank accounts. Exact matching existing journal entries can be linked without new bank postings. Source amount/date/unit/reference remain locked.
5. Verified completed bank transfer posting with two bank sides and separate reconciliation navigation. No actual money transfer.
6. Balanced journal CSV batch preview and atomic posting with duplicate, company, account, unit and closed-period guards.
7. Native trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, GL, bank activity, check/withdrawal register, deposit register and journal register with CSV/PDF exports and account/entry drilldowns.
8. Company financial-account overview, source-to-bank reference matching, payables exceptions and monthly-close controls.

Browser QA used synthetic in-memory records only: itemized bill approval/posting and $40 partial settlement of a $100 bill; linking an existing $40 tenant receipt without another deposit; completed $25 transfer; balanced $10 journal batch; recurring template creation; bulk two-line $50 bill import and approval. Trial balance debit/credit totals matched with zero difference. Temporary QA routes are removed before release.

Reviewed reference applications: AppFolio Devonshire Condominiums HOA and QBO Devonshire Leasing Agency Inc. These are different company contexts. No AppFolio financial records were copied into OG and no external accounting/payment records were changed.

Remaining AppFolio parity work:
- Native homeowner/owner/vendor/other receipts and charges, subsidy/application-fee flows, bulk/common charges, late fees and lockbox/debt-collection integrations.
- Imported supplier-document conversion into native AP with source linkage; Smart Bill/OCR, board-specific approvals, management-fee/owner-draw batches, loan schedules and loan servicing.
- Grouped bank deposit workflow, credit-card account reconciliation, live bank feeds/rules and bank authorization lifecycle.
- Gross potential rent posting, advanced account map/GL permissions and specialized security deposit/prepayment diagnostics.
- Provider-specific ACH/card collection, eCheck fees and live online bank authorization. Provider selection/connection is deferred by the user.
- Advanced reports, tax/1099 workflows and automatic external-system synchronization.

Financial limits: existing source registers are evidence unless explicitly posted to the native company ledger; reports label that scope. Importing/approving documents does not issue checks, transfer money, or charge ACH/cards. Production use still requires verified company opening balances and reviewed account mapping.
