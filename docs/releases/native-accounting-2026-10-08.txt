OG NATIVE ACCOUNTING — 8 OCTOBER 2026

IMPLEMENTED
Journal entries > Native ledger: verified balanced company openings (including explicit zero openings), reviewed native journal postings, exact one-time reversal entries, debit/credit trial balance, cumulative financial review PDF, trial balance PDF/CSV, clickable GL amounts and exact journal/unit details.
Journal entries > Recurring journals: monthly balanced templates with start/end dates, calendar-day clamping, pause/resume history and explicit monthly review/posting. Template/month duplicate posting is rejected. No automatic postings or QBO adjustments.
Financial accounts > Statement reconciliation: CSV/manual bank statement rows, positive deposit/negative withdrawal convention, matching one or more ledger entries to each bank row, verified prior balance/date continuity, outstanding deposit/check carry-forward, zero statement and adjusted-bank differences, immutable completed history and reconciliation PDF. Backdated bank postings to reconciled periods are blocked; corrections must be later entries.
Online payments > Provider setup: universal future provider name, clean HTTPS dashboard link, ACH/card preference and disable control. User explicitly chose to keep the provider connection ready. Live payment collection remains disabled until a processor-specific checkout/credential/settlement integration is implemented and tested. Dashboard access alone is not a payment integration.

FINANCIAL AND ACCESS CONTROLS
Records remain in the authenticated client's existing workspace and revision-controlled save path. Only existing authorized management roles can save. Server stamps new books, journals, templates, reconciliations and provider setup records with the authenticated reviewer and server time. No schema changes or broader access permissions were introduced. Read-only/scoped portals do not receive native ledger data.
Balanced journal lines, safe integer cents, company chart matching, immutable history, duplicate journal references/external transaction IDs, exact reversals and closed-month guards are validated on save. Native entries and reconciliation records participate in monthly-close fingerprints. Native Bank accounts require completed statement reconciliation through month end before closing.

LIMITS
Native books support explicitly reviewed double-entry postings and statement reconciliation. This release does not automatically convert all existing OG/QBO source records to GL entries and does not provide live bank feeds. Financial review reports contain only verified native openings and explicit native postings. Complete AppFolio parity (advanced bills/approvals, recurring bills, bulk charges, asset/prepaid accounting and automated settlement/refund flows) remains future development. Payment-provider activation is intentionally deferred by the user.
