# Spatial headquarters and AI role directory

The office uses CSS3D perspective, depth, desk and avatar geometry. Users can rotate the campus, choose a flat plan, select departments, search profiles and inspect each role's single supervisor and direct reports. There is one human Founder/CEO and32 fictional AI display names, including10 executive profiles overall. AI Resources & Capacity replaces HR and covers capacity planning, orchestration, knowledge and evaluation. The reporting graph has no cycles and no manager exceeds5 direct reports. Product reports through Technology; strategy, secretariat and audit report directly to the human CEO.

These profiles do not provision identities, permissions, paid resources or independent execution. AI legal/finance roles support qualified human oversight. No real client assignment, service commitment or business result is inferred.

A trusted private activity producer can include agentId matching a roster ID. The protected workforce API allowlists this optional field. Only fresh Working records whose agentId and office both match a non-human profile permit individual animation. Old role-only department records remain department evidence and never get attributed to a named profile. Stale/disconnected data and reduced motion suppress activity animation. Human CEO never gets AI-work animation.

No writer or autonomous cloud executor is added here. Real account setup, protected feed provisioning, client separation and production releases retain their existing approvals. The generic roster lives in lib/ai-workforce.ts; do not add client records or credentials to public source.
